# User guide

A short guide for the people who use the system every day.

## First day

1. **Setup > Company**: write the company name, phone and address. They are printed on bills.
2. **Setup > Exchange rates**: write today's rate for AFN and PKR ("1 USD = how many?").
   Add a new rate whenever it changes. The system always uses the newest rate up to the date of a paper.
3. **Setup > Warehouses**: add your warehouses.
4. **Stock > Products** and **Raw materials**: add what you sell and what you buy.
5. **Customers**, **Suppliers**, **Banks, sarafi, cash**: add them. If someone already owes you
   money (or you owe them), write it in "Balance today" when you add them.
6. **Stock > Opening stock**: write what is in each warehouse today and what each item cost.
7. **Users > Roles** and **Users**: give every person their own user and a role.

## Every day

| I want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Sell goods | **Sales > New sale**. Choose the customer and the items. Write the money received, if any. Print the bill. |
| Give a price offer | **Sales > Quotations**. Later press "Turn into a sale". |
| Send the goods | Open the sale, section **Delivery**: On the way, then Delivered. |
| Buy goods | **Purchases > New purchase**. Add transport and customs under "Other costs". Switch off "The goods have arrived" if they are still on the way. |
| Receive goods that were on the way | **Purchases > Goods on the way**, open the purchase, press "Goods received". |
| Receive money from a customer | **Money > Money in**. |
| Pay a supplier, or give an advance | **Money > Money out**. |
| Pay rent, electricity, fuel | **Money > Expense**. Expense names are under Banks, sarafi, cash > Expense categories. |
| Move money between cash, bank and sarafi, or exchange currency | **Money > Transfer / exchange**. |
| See what someone owes | Open **Customers** or **Suppliers** and press **Statement**. |
| See what is in the warehouse | **Stock > Stock on hand**. |
| See everything recorded today | **Money > Day book (roznamcha)**. |

## Every month

- **Employees > Payroll**: choose the month, check the list and save. Then pay each employee
  with the "Pay" button. Advances taken during the month are taken off by themselves.
- **Assets**: press "Write off this month" once.
- **Stock > Physical count**: count a warehouse and write what you found.
- **Reports**: Profit and loss, Who owes us and whom we owe, Cash flow.

## Good to know

- **A mistake?** Papers are not edited. Open the paper, press **Cancel**, and enter it again.
  The cancelled paper stays in the list so nothing is hidden.
- **Dates**: press your name at the top right to switch between the Gregorian and the
  Solar Hijri calendar. It only changes what you see.
- **Currency**: every customer, supplier, bank and cash box has one currency. A bill is
  written in the currency of the customer or supplier.
- **Free items** on a bill: write price 0.
- **Not enough stock**: the system refuses to sell more than there is. Check Stock on hand,
  or record the purchase or the opening stock first.
- **Files**: a photo or scan can be attached to every sale and purchase, and kept under Documents.
